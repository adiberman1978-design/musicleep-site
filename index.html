Musicleep

Musicleep is a personal, single-user tool that a musician runs on his own computer to make music videos for his own songs.

When a clip is finished, Musicleep uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, as a private video, together with its title, description, thumbnail and captions. The clip becomes visible only when the owner chooses to publish it.

Musicleep is not offered to the public. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no other users.
